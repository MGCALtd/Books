# Appendix K. Release board questions to print

Use this sheet for one candidate and one proposed next state. Write evidence references beside answers; use “unknown” when the evidence is missing. The companion includes an editable copy.

Candidate/manifest: ____________________  Contract: ____________________

Proposed next state: ____________________  Decision owner: ______________

1. VALUE. Who can complete which useful task, and how does this compare with the existing route?

2. BOUNDARY. Which effects and data paths are prohibited? Where are they enforced outside the prompt?

3. EVIDENCE. Which cases, trials, graders and accepted-state observations support each promise?

4. FINDINGS. Which severe defects, ordinary defects, unknowns and exceptions remain? Who owns them?

5. AUTHORITY. Does the actual caller, tool, target, payload, operation and current approval match at execution?

6. RECOVERY. What happens before commit, after a lost reply and when a remote effect remains uncertain?

7. OPERATIONS. What are the time, call, cost and reviewer-capacity limits? What does the user see at exhaustion?

8. HANDLING. Who may see the evidence, where is restricted content retained, and when is it removed?

9. STOP. Who can stop the interface AND the effect path? How is accepted work preserved and reconciled?

10. CHANGE. When does this decision expire, and which source, policy, credential, tool or purpose change triggers review?
